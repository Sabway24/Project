About Us

EcoTrackBin was established in 2025 by a group of UTP students to develop a Smart Bin product with a waste management strategy for cleaner and sustainable cities.

We focus on providing real-time monitoring systems, user-friendly web dashboards, GPS-based bin tracking, and LED alert systems. Our team is committed to delivering high-quality products and services for your satisfaction.
